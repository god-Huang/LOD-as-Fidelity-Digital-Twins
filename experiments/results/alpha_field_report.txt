consequences for the manuscript: (a) geometric and friction error are comparable in DECISION terms at the coarsest level (0.330 against 0.30-0.35) while geometry dominates by an order of magnitude in the ENERGY norm (0.141 against 0.066-0.087); (b) spatial correlation reduces the decision count, contrary to the intuition that white noise is the mildest admissible perturbation.
